\caption{Real logged comparison between a GRPO-only run and an OPSD run. (a) GRPO supplies one caption-level outcome at each training stage; the centered stage mean is broadcast over the six localization-rollout columns, so all columns receive the same scalar credit. (b) OPSD preserves the six measured localization IoUs for each teacher-diagnosis record, subtracts that record's six-rollout mean, and aggregates the residuals by stage; positive and negative cells expose within-caption evidence variation that the GRPO panel collapses. This is the visible supervision distinction: OPSD can retain which rollout evidence is strong or weak instead of assigning one value to the whole row. (c) The upper trace compares measured mean pixel IoU over normalized progress with rollout-spread bands; the lower stack shows OPSD's regenerate/privileged-JSD/original-GRPO route shares against the GRPO 100\% scalar-route baseline. Because the runs do not share a common checkpoint or fixed rollout, this remains descriptive training dynamics rather than a causal loss ablation, token-autograd map, or before/after parameter-update measurement.}
