Token-update mechanism diagnostic on a held-out direct RefCOCO sample.  Each point is one of the two legal
SAMTok mask-code tokens in a ground-truth target sequence.  The horizontal coordinate is teacher alignment,
$\log p_{\mathrm{direct\ teacher}}(y_t\mid s_t)-\log p_{\mathrm{base}}(y_t\mid s_t)$, and is used only as a
mechanistic proxy.  The vertical coordinate is the measured checkpoint change,
$\log p_{\mathrm{checkpoint}}(y_t\mid s_t)-\log p_{\mathrm{base}}(y_t\mid s_t)$, evaluated by a second
teacher-forced forward pass.  The two panels use identical points, limits, and masks.  The 3-D view adds
$\exp(\mathrm{mean}_t\log p_{\mathrm{base}}(y_t\mid s_t))$ as a bounded target-sequence reliability proxy.
The diagnostic uses 16 rows sampled with seed 20260921 from a RefCOCO parquet disjoint from the documented 20k
cycle parquet; 32 mask-code positions are expected, subject to finite-value filtering.  The GRPO and Pixel-OPSD
checkpoints were historical one-epoch runs from the same SAMTok base but used different training parquets, so this
figure is not a controlled paired objective ablation and does not establish causal token credit or benchmark gains.
